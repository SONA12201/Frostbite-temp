About Frostbite

Keeping Temperature-Sensitive Cargo in Focus

Frostbite is a proposed cold-chain logistics business concept designed to support the movement and storage of products that require controlled temperatures. The aim is to connect careful handling, shipment visibility, and planned delivery.

  • What we do: Cold-chain transport, storage coordination, packaging and monitoring concepts.
  • Target customers: Pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, laboratories, food businesses and life-science companies.
  • Service area: India-focused concept; actual operating locations to be confirmed.
  • Value proposition: Product-focused handling, clear shipment information and coordinated logistics.

Protecting Product Integrity

A cold chain is more than a refrigerated vehicle. It requires the right temperature profile, suitable packaging, careful handling, records, and an action plan for unexpected conditions.
